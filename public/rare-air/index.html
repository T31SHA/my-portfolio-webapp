Day 03 · The threshold

Every gompa begins with a gate.

Carved cedar, prayer flags bleached by altitude. You arrive on foot, the way pilgrims have for nine centuries.

Day 04 · Morning puja

Inside, the day is already old.

Monks cross the courtyard before the sun clears the ridge. We stay two nights, long enough to stop being visitors.
